Sam's training plan

Execution notes

Rest
~90 sec on compounds. 60 sec on isolation. Keep density high — don't drag rest while cutting.
RPE Target
RPE 7–8 on most working sets — finish with 2–3 reps in the tank. Last set can push to RPE 9 (1 rep left).
Progression
During a cut, the goal is to not lose strength. Add 2.5–5 lbs every 1–2 weeks on big lifts. Hit top of rep range with good form for 2 sessions in a row → bump weight.
The Primer
Non-negotiable. Six minutes of dead bug / bird dog / plank protects your low back on squats and rows.
Track These
Top set on goblet squat, DB bench, hip thrust, lat pulldown, seated row. If those hold or climb while bodyweight drops — you're recomping.
